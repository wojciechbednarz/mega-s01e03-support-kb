0002 · Duplicates share a cause, not a subject line

owner Wojciech Bednarz accepted reviewed 2026-10-30 · 0 days ago

Date: 30 October 2026 · Deciders: support desk · Replaces: 0001

Context

Decision 0001 merged reports whose subject lines matched. #4471 shows what that costs: 412 reports with 12 different subjects ended up in one ticket marked known, and they hold three problems with three fixes (#4471 is three problems, not one). People describe different bugs in the same words.

Decision

Two reports are the same issue when they have the same cause, worked out from the tenant (its locale and booking form) and the channel, as causeOf in src/lib/kb/causes.ts does. A report whose body shows a different mechanism (a one-day move, not a swap) goes to its own ticket even when its tenant points elsewhere. The subject line and the intake tag are hints, never the key.

Each ticket a big ticket is split into is listed in src/lib/data/tickets.json, so the incident page shows where every cause is tracked.

Consequences

  • Triage reads the tenant and the channel, not just the subject. Slower on an incident morning.
  • A queued fix closes only its own cause’s ticket. The rest stay open.
  • 0001 stays as written; this record replaces it.
